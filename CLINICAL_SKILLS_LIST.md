# EMS Competency Tracker - Clinical Skills List

This document contains a complete list of all clinical skills and procedures that can be recorded in the EMS Competency Tracker system.

**Total Skills Categories: 9**
**Total Individual Skills: 160+**

---

## 1. VITAL SIGNS (VitalSignEntry)

### Cardiovascular Measurements
- Blood Pressure (Manual, NIBP Auto Cuff, Palpation, Doppler, Arterial Line)
- Heart Rate
- Pulse Strength/Quality (Absent, Irregular Strong, Irregular Weak, Regular Strong, Regular Weak, Thready, Bounding)
- Orthostatic BP & HR (Lying & Standing)

### Respiratory Measurements
- Respirations Rate
- Respirations Quality (Normal, Shallow, Deep, Labored, Gasping, Kussmaul, Cheyne-Stokes, Apneic)
- SpO2 (Oxygen Saturation)
- End-Tidal CO2 (ETCO2)
- Peak Flow (Pre & Post Treatment)

### Neurological Assessments
- Pain Scale (0-10)
- Pupil Size (1mm-8mm, Pinpoint, Midpoint, Dilated)
- Pupil Reaction (Brisk, Sluggish, Fixed)
- GCS Score (Eyes, Verbal, Motor)

### Other Vital Signs
- Temperature (Oral, Axillary, Tympanic, Rectal, Esophageal, Skin, Temporal Artery, Forehead, Core)
- Blood Glucose Level (Glucometer, Visual Strip, Lab Value)
- Skin Condition Assessment
- Lung Sounds Assessment
- APGAR Score (Neonatal)
